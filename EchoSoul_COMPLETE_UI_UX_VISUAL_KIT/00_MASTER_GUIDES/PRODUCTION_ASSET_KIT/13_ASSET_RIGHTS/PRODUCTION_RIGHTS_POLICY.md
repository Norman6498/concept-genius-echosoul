# Asset Rights Policy

Reference screenshots and generated concept boards are design references, not automatically cleared production assets.

Production assets must be:
1. created specifically for EchoSoul with a commercial-use-compatible generation/licensing arrangement,
2. properly licensed stock/commissioned artwork,
3. open-license assets whose license permits the intended use.

For literature, store provenance and rights evidence separately from UI assets.
Do not ship copyrighted full book pages/poems without rights.
Do not use user-generated memories as advertising targeting data.
