# EchoSoul — Literary Animation Motion Specification

## Objective
The literary experience should feel like discovering a line in a quiet book, then watching the words come alive on the page.

## Visual sequence
1. Paper canvas settles in.
2. Subtle page/camera movement.
3. Writing instrument/ink begins.
4. Characters appear progressively in authentic order.
5. Natural pauses at punctuation and line breaks.
6. Final line finishes.
7. Author/title/source fades into readable attribution.

## Modes
- Ink pen
- Pencil
- Brush/calligraphic feel
- Minimal handwriting

Choose typography based on script. Hindi/Devanagari, Urdu, and Latin scripts must use appropriate fonts and shaping; never force one handwriting font across all languages.

## Controls
Replay, pause, speed, read aloud, save, another match.

## Integrity constraint
Animation is presentation only. The renderer has no authority to alter source content.
