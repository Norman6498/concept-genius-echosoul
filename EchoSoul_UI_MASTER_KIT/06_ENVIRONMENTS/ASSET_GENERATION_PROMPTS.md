# EchoSoul Asset Generation Prompt Templates

Use these prompts with an image-generation workflow to create CLEAN assets, not UI screenshots.

## Global visual DNA
Cinematic literary environment; deep navy/charcoal shadows; warm ivory highlights; restrained muted gold; editorial fantasy realism; cozy natural materials; atmospheric depth; premium consumer technology; no text; no UI; no logos; no watermark; no people unless specifically requested.

## Environment prompt template
Create a clean cinematic environment for EchoSoul, [ENVIRONMENT], [TIME OF DAY], [WEATHER].
Composition must leave a quiet low-contrast region for UI overlay.
Create separately usable foreground, midground and background elements.
Natural lighting, volumetric atmosphere, subtle depth, realistic material response.
No text, no interface, no logo, no watermark.

## Time-of-day variants
Early morning: pale sunrise, cool mist, soft amber horizon, quiet atmosphere.
Morning: clean daylight, fresh foliage, crisp sky, energetic but calm.
Late morning: clear balanced daylight, slightly stronger shadows, productive atmosphere.
Afternoon: warm natural light, lively sky, richer foliage, active atmosphere.
Evening: golden-hour light, long shadows, first lamps beginning to glow.
Night: deep blue sky, moonlight, warm lamps, stars, intimate atmosphere.
Late night: darker blue/indigo, sparse light, slower quiet feeling, dreamy atmosphere.

## Page-specific environment prompts
Landing: cinematic window/landscape realm, inviting and expansive.
Home: personalized cozy room opening to a living landscape.
Talk: quiet private room, soft lamp, rain/window or calm night exterior.
Music: atmospheric listening room with subtle visual rhythm.
Words: literary reading room, paper/books, window, moon/rain, soft lamp.
Scenes: environment must match selected need; visual + sound implied, no UI.
Moments: desk/journal, window, books, personal keepsakes, warm light.
Journey: mountain path with glowing route and milestone lights.
Life Novel: magical literary study/library with floating pages and warm light.
Settings: calm control-room version of the EchoSoul realm.
Premium: richer cinematic world with deeper depth and more elaborate atmosphere.
Pricing: public-facing cinematic realm, cleaner composition with marketing-safe negative space.

sky / clouds / distant landscape / midground foliage / foreground foliage / architecture / lamps / lamp glow / particles / mist / weather as separate transparent assets.
